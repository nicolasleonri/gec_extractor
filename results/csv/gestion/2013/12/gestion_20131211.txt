According to Alfredo Galindo, commercial manager of Pension Vitalicias at Pacific Life Insurance, today merely five percent of pensions are disbursed in USD. \"There exists belief among some individuals anticipating recovery of the dollar value across three to four coming years,\" he noted. Currently, eight-tenths favor life insurance policies as preferred modes of post-retirement income security, though twenty-percent select structured withdrawal plans—a trend increasing recently.\nLife insurances offer beneficiaries stable lifelong payouts through company-provided annuity schemes. Among various types offered, those including guaranteed periods garner significant attention. With ninety percent opting for such secured lifetime benefits, majority preference leans towards extended guarantee durations beyond fifteen-year spans even after policyholder demise ensuring familial financial support continuity despite insurer retention risks associated with non-guaranteed deferred-period models offering higher initial payments compared against subsequent assured amounts.\nWednesday, Nov 13th edition of Oficina Financiera reports market predictions indicating annual growth reaching \$526 million for life-insurance products alongside programmed withdrawals expected to hit \$126 million during current year projections.\nForecast anticipates combined totals nearing \$597 million come 2014 based on estimates provided by Pacific Life Insurance.
